As summarized in this survey, DORA's 2025 research covered roughly 5,000 professionals and found 90% using AI at work. AI was associated with higher delivery throughput, alongside continuing instability.

The survey draws a systems lesson: architecture, documentation, version control, feedback loops and a clear AI policy help determine the result. Faster generation alone cannot repair a delivery process. Small batches and reliable engineering foundations make the added capacity more useful.
