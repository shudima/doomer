AI can synthesize customer feedback, analytics, support history and backlog data into planning options. Read-only codebase analysis can identify affected services, dependencies and migration risks. A working spike can make a proposal easier to evaluate.

The survey recommends a maintained product-context corpus and AI-assisted premortems. Keep people responsible for roadmap decisions. Cheaper boilerplate and tests do not make ambiguity, integration or **verification** disappear; stale context and fabricated market evidence can still produce a convincing bad plan.
