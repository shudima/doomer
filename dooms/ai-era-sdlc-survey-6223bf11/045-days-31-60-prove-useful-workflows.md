The survey's second month introduces specification and ticket templates, readiness checks and a background-agent pilot on clear tasks. Add CI failure triage, require a regression test with fixes and filter generated tests for reliability and value.

For every LLM feature, begin evaluation work with failure analysis. The proposed exit criteria are at least 15% of merged pull requests being agent-authored with rework no worse than baseline, plus an evaluation gate on one AI feature. Quality remains part of the adoption threshold.
