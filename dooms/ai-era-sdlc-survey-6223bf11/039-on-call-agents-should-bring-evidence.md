AI reliability agents can correlate metrics, logs, traces and recent deployments as soon as an alert fires. The survey recommends an evidence pack: suspicious changes, graphs, similar incidents, likely owners and relevant runbook steps.

These tools can draft timelines and postmortems while people evaluate competing causes. A confident root-cause claim can anchor responders incorrectly. Keep hypotheses grounded in evidence, and treat log lines and alert payloads as untrusted material rather than instructions an agent should obey.
