An agent needs a ticket that explains what successful work looks like. Include:

- Context and the goal.
- Acceptance criteria and what is outside scope.
- Relevant files or examples.
- A test plan and a definition of done.

A failing test or useful log can explain more than generic prose. Keep the ticket, specification and pull request linked. Treat instructions embedded in ticket content as untrusted input.
