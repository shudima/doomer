Before refactoring legacy software, the survey recommends characterization tests that pin current behavior. Agents can help document unfamiliar systems, answer codebase questions and perform incremental modernization. Human review still matters when existing behavior contains a defect.

Schedule small dependency, documentation and flaky-test cleanup changes rather than letting chores accumulate. The survey offers a 20% technical-debt budget as an example, not a universal target. Keep people learning the system while agents help maintain it, or comprehension debt can grow beneath tidy changes.
