At the survey's orchestrated level, background agents take scoped tickets toward pull requests inside sandboxes. Teams share skills, plan reviewer capacity and connect mutation testing, evaluation gates, incident evidence and bug-fix pipelines. Cost is tracked per outcome.

At the AI-native level, humans define intent and constraints while agents help implement, verify and maintain. Continuous evaluations and bounded remediation support operation. Retrospectives improve agent context. The model describes organizational practice across the lifecycle, beyond the sophistication of a coding tool.
