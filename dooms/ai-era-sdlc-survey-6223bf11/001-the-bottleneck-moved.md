AI can produce code quickly. Delivering useful software still takes review, tests, security checks and human understanding.

This survey argues that those steps increasingly limit the pace of delivery. Its advice starts before generation: explain the goal, provide the right context and build enough capacity to check the result. Strong engineering practices help a team use the extra output; weak practices can amplify existing problems.
