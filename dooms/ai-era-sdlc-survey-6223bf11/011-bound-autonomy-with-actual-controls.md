The survey highlights a dangerous combination: private data, untrusted content and a way to communicate externally. **Prompt injection** hides instructions in material an agent reads; tool poisoning can hide them in tool descriptions or schemas.

Its response is concrete: isolate execution, narrow credentials, control network access, keep audit logs and require approval at consequential boundaries. Written rules help explain intent. Sandboxes and permissions constrain what an agent can actually do when those rules fail.
