**Illustrative example**

An agent generates a new export feature, a refactor and a dependency upgrade in one pull request. The reviewer struggles to tell which behavior changed.

Split the unrelated work into small changes. Review the export against its acceptance criteria, then assess the refactor and upgrade separately. Faster generation becomes useful when people can still understand and verify each change.
