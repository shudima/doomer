The survey recommends deployment frequency, change lead time, pull-request cycle time and time to first review alongside failure rate, rework, escaped defects, churn and security findings. Review quality trends monthly and quarterly because many outcomes lag behind activity.

Also track change-size distribution, reviewer load, CI duration and flake rate. These reveal where authoring gains disappear into queues. A balanced view can show whether faster coding produces reliable delivery or simply transfers work to the next person.
