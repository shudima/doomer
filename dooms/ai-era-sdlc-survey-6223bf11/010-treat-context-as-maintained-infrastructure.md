**Context engineering** means choosing what the model sees: instructions, examples, relevant files and tool results.

Keep repository conventions in files such as `AGENTS.md`. Put recurring procedures in skills. Use retrieval to bring in details when needed; MCP can connect tools and data.

Version and review this material like code. Irrelevant context takes space and can make the important information harder to use.
