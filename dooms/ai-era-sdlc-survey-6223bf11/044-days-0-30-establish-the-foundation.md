The survey's first month establishes an AI policy covering data, licensing and security, selects approved tools and connectors, and adds shared agent guidance to important repositories. AI first-pass review, static security analysis and secret scanning enter the pull-request workflow.

Baseline delivery metrics, review time, change size and rework before judging improvement. The proposed exit criteria are a working baseline dashboard and more than 70% weekly use of approved tools. That adoption target belongs to the survey's roadmap; usage alone does not prove value.
