The survey cites METR's July 2025 randomized trial: 16 experienced open-source developers completed 246 tasks in mature repositories they knew well. With early-2025 AI tools, they were 19% slower while believing they were 20% faster. Fewer than 44% of generations were accepted.

This is a scoped result about particular developers, tasks and tools. The practical lesson is: measure completed work and quality alongside perceived speed. Confidence alone cannot establish a productivity gain.
