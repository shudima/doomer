The survey's five-level maturity model begins with ad hoc individual chat and autocomplete use, no shared rules and no AI policy. Assisted teams add shared tools and a policy, but often measure usage rather than impact.

At the agentic level, shared repository instructions, small pull requests and AI first-pass review become normal. Teams add filtered test generation, CI triage, clear specifications and readiness checks for tickets. Delivery, review time and rework become part of the assessment.
