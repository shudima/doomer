The survey describes browser test agents that plan scenarios, generate code and repair broken tests. These can help explore an application, audit accessibility and sweep across browsers. QA engineers still own risk selection, test data and the quality of the agents' instructions.

Start test cases from acceptance criteria and turn useful exploratory findings into repeatable checks. Treat self-healing cautiously: the survey notes limited independent evidence beyond locator repair. Humans should review repairs that might change what a test actually verifies.
