Write requirements that someone can test, and link them to real user evidence. A versioned specification can describe the goal, design and tasks.

Templates such as **Given/When/Then** or EARS express conditions and expected behavior. The survey gives Spec Kit and Kiro as tooling examples.

Ask AI to find ambiguity and missing constraints, including privacy, accessibility and latency. Keep the specification short and current as the implementation changes.
