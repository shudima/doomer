The survey recommends silent human ideation before AI expands or clusters ideas, reducing the chance that the model's first answer anchors everyone. Pre-reads can reserve meeting time for decisions; asynchronous digests can replace some status meetings.

Capture the decision, owner, rationale and date, then draft linked action tickets for human approval. Check summaries for disagreement flattened into false consensus. Recording consent, sensitive information and duplicate action items need deliberate handling even when transcription is automatic.
