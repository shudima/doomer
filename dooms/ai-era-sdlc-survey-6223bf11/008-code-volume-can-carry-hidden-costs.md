The survey cites GitClear's vendor analysis of 153 million changed lines: churn and copy-paste duplication rose while refactoring declined as assistants spread. That observation flags maintainability risk; it does not establish that every AI change causes decay.

It also summarizes Veracode's vendor benchmark of 80 tasks across more than 100 models: 45% of tasks produced insecure code, with no improvement from model size. The survey recommends explicit refactoring work and security checks on every generated change.
