A mixed human-agent sprint needs more than a count of coding tasks. The survey recommends routing tickets to a human, an agent or a pair according to specification clarity, blast radius and nearby test coverage.

Scan dependencies across planned work and budget reviewer hours explicitly. Twenty generated pull requests still create twenty reviews. Rebaseline velocity after AI adoption rather than comparing story points across the change, and keep sprint commitments tied to outcomes that matter.
