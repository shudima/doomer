The survey recommends deterministic checks first, an AI review next, author fixes after that and a human review focused on intent, design, security and maintainability. Repository guidance and past incident lessons can make automated comments more relevant.

AI review can catch simple bugs, missing tests and documentation gaps. The survey warns that business-logic errors, authorization flaws and races remain difficult. Tune noisy categories and measure review **latency** and depth, so automated comments support attention instead of exhausting it.
