AI can prepare a retrospective brief from cycle time, pull-request size, review delays, rework and incidents. The survey recommends using that material to generate hypotheses for discussion, then mining several retrospectives for recurring unresolved themes.

Turn lessons into changes to repository guidance, skills, review checklists and evaluation cases. Track actions through completion. Blameless discussion, candor and team ownership remain human work. Metrics used for individual surveillance can damage the learning loop the retrospective is meant to create.
