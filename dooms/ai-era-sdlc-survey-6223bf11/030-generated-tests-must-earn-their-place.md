Keep generated tests that build, pass repeatedly and add useful coverage or catch a plausible fault. The survey connects this to Meta’s assured-generation approach and human review.

Inspect the assertion and the mocks. A test can encode an existing bug as the expected result, or mock away the behavior it claims to check. A large suite of weak tests can become slower and harder to maintain without protecting the product.
