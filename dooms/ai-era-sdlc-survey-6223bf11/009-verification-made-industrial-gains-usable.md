The industrial examples in the survey combine generation with filters. Meta's TestGen-LLM reported 75% of generated tests building, 57% passing reliably and 25% increasing coverage; engineers accepted 73% of suggestions.

At Google, ML-suggested edits resolved 7.5% of reviewer comments. A separate migration study reported about 50% faster work with roughly 74% of changed code generated. These are distinct deployments. Their shared lesson is to choose verifiable tasks and evaluate the resulting changes.
