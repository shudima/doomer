A bad paragraph is one kind of failure. Blocking an account, paying an invoice, approving a transaction or shutting down a server creates a different security problem.

The presenter asks two questions about those decisions: can we recognize when the AI is wrong, and can we tell whether someone caused it to be wrong? Action-taking systems need answers to both.
