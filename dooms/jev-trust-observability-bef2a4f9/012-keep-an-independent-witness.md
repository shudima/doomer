Human escalation and independent **verification** matter for high-risk decisions. The video recommends keeping people involved where the consequences justify another check.

Just as crucially, Jev should not be the only component that knows what happened. Records and observations outside the model let someone investigate an action later, instead of relying entirely on the same component whose judgment is being questioned.
