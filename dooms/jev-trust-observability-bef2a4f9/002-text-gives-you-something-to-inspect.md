An LLM’s response may be wrong, even confidently wrong. Still, the text gives a reviewer something to challenge, compare, or investigate alongside the tools and information used in the workflow.

The presenter values those observable artifacts without calling them perfect. A well-worded explanation can itself be bogus; having something to inspect is only the beginning of checking a decision.
