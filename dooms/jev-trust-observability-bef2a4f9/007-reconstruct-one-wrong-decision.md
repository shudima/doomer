**Illustrative example**

A sorter archived an urgent email. Inspect the received message, extracted features, model version, confidence and action record.

You may discover that the model never saw the deadline because preprocessing removed it. Without that trail, changing the confidence threshold could hide the real cause. Observe both what reached the model and what happened afterward.
