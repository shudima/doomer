The concern is not that Jev must be less accurate than an LLM, or that probabilistic decisions are inherently bad. The presenter finds the structured approach compelling.

The conclusion is about responsibility: as AI makes more decisions for us, those decisions must become observable, auditable and verifiable. A probability is useful, but it is not an explanation or proof that the action deserves trust.
