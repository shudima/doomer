The presenter highlights TypeSafe’s suggestion to ask several smaller questions instead of one large question. For a security alert, ask about authorization, supporting evidence and risk before deciding the response.

This makes intermediate judgments observable. Engineers can inspect how those pieces contributed to the final action, rather than treating a single recommendation as an indivisible black box. Decomposition improves the surrounding design.
