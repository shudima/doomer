A probability is one input to a trust decision. It does not decide which actions a system should be allowed to take.

The discussion separates model confidence from the surrounding policy: evidence, permissions, checks and consequences still matter. A confident prediction can guide a low-risk choice while an expensive or irreversible action needs stronger verification.
