The article’s design checklist combines recoverable offloading, **reversible** compaction, on-demand instructions, isolated noisy work, and memory in storage you control. Each technique reduces the active burden without requiring the same information to be lost.

Tune thresholds and defaults for the task. Context management also complements retrieval: the harness must both find useful information and manage the history produced while working with it.
