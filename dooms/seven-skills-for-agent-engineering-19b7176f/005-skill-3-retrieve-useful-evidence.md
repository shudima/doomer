**Retrieval**-augmented generation brings relevant documents into the model’s context instead of relying only on what it learned during training. The quality of that evidence limits what the agent can do with it.

The presenter warns that irrelevant material can still produce a confident answer. Retrieval is therefore part of the engineering problem, rather than a simple attachment step before the model does the supposedly important work.
