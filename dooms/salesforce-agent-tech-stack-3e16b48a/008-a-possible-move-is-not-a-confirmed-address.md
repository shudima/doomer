**Illustrative example**

A customer asks whether a plan works in Florida. The assistant may remember that a move could be relevant.

It should not overwrite the customer’s current address with Florida. Ask for confirmation before turning an inferred preference or possibility into an authoritative profile fact. Memory and verified business records have different roles.
