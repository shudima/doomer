Anthropic reports that most Code Reviews now finish about three times faster than during preview, moving from roughly twenty minutes to six to ten minutes.

It also says findings are clearer and focus on bugs that matter, explaining the problem, its trigger, and the fix in a few sentences. These are the vendor’s product claims in the announcement, rather than independently reproduced timing or bug-detection measurements.
