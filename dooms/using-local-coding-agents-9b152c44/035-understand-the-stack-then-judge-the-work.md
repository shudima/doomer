Raschka’s central lesson is broader than installing a particular tool. Understand the model-serving layer, the agent harness, its permission model, and how to evaluate the work it actually produces.

He still rates leading hosted models above smaller local ones, while finding newer 30–35B mixture-of-experts models capable enough for many tasks. The useful comparison is whether a particular local combination meets your requirements for reliability, responsiveness, control, and the machine you have.
