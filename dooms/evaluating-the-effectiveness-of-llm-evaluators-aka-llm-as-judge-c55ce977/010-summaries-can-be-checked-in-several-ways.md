The summarization study reviewed by Yan compares numerical scoring, pairwise preferences, semantic content coverage and sentence-level factuality checks. These approaches ask different questions about the same generated summary.

A summary can sound fluent while omitting important content or introducing unsupported facts. Separating those criteria makes the evaluation easier to interpret than compressing all quality into one unexplained score.
