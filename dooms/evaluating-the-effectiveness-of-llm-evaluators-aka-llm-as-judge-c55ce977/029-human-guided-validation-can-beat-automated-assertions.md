In the reviewed product task, EvalGen caught more user-identified defects than a fully automated assertion baseline. Participants also added criteria as they encountered new bad responses.

Some trusted editable code assertions more readily than model judgments. Yan highlights a useful division of labor: people supply and refine the quality standard, while tools help implement it and scale its application.
