The Shepherd study used both people and another language model to assess critiques. Numerical ratings sometimes disagreed substantially, and the model judge favored feedback with more examples.

Yan uses this result to warn about verbosity and scoring biases. A critic that receives high ratings from another model has not automatically demonstrated the same improvement to human reviewers or end users.
