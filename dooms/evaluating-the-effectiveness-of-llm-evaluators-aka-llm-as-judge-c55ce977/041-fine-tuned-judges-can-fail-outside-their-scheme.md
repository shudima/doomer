The limitations study reviewed by Yan finds strong in-domain results alongside weak transfer to other evaluation schemes and difficult fairness, factuality or safety tasks. Some specialized judges behave more like task-specific classifiers than general evaluators.

The lesson is not that fine-tuning is useless. It is that competence on the training distribution should not be confused with broad, portable judgment.
