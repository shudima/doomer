Research surveyed by Yan generally found pairwise comparisons closer to human preferences than direct scoring for the tested subjective tasks. Factual consistency did not show the same clear improvement.

The lesson is to preserve the match between method and requirement. Better preference alignment on writing quality is not evidence that pairwise judging becomes the best way to enforce factual support.
