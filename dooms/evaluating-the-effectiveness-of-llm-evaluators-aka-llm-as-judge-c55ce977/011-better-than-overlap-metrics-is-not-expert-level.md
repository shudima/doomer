One study found a language-model judge more aligned with people than several conventional summary metrics, yet substantially less aligned than human experts were with one another.

Yan also notes that reported binary factuality accuracy omitted **precision** and recall. A promising aggregate result therefore did not establish how well the evaluator detected inconsistent summaries or avoided false alarms.
