**Illustrative example**

A judge sees response A first and chooses it. Swap the slots while keeping both texts unchanged. It now chooses the other response.

That reversal suggests a position effect rather than a stable preference. Record the disagreement and investigate before treating the verdict as a training label or a release decision.
