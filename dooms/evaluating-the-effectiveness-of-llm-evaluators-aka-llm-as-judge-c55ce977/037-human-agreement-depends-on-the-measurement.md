Some chatbot-evaluation studies report model-human agreement comparable to or higher than human-human agreement. Yan cautions that the setup, treatment of ties and agreement metric affect that interpretation.

An agreement percentage does not correct for coincidental agreement. Promising results on open-ended preferences should therefore be read with their measurement conditions, rather than generalized into a claim that model judgment has universally surpassed experts.
