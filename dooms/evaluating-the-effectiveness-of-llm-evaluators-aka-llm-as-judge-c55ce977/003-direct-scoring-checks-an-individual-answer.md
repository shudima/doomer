**Direct scoring** evaluates one answer against a criterion. That might be pass/fail or a rating on a defined scale.

The criterion needs to be clear enough to apply consistently. Asking whether an answer is supported by a supplied passage is a different task from asking whether it sounds helpful. Decide what the score means before treating it as evidence of quality.
