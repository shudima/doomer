For subjective qualities such as tone or persuasiveness, choosing between two responses can produce more stable human-aligned judgments than assigning each an independent numerical score.

Yan treats this as task-dependent rather than universally superior. Pairwise evaluation needs an alternative, should allow ties and does not replace an absolute factuality check merely because it identifies which response sounds better.
