A pairwise judge can be influenced by where a response appears or how long it is. The preferred answer may change even when the useful content does not.

Swap the response order and test unnecessary length changes. Check whether the same underlying answer still wins. Yan treats these biases as reasons to validate the comparison procedure, rather than trusting a fluent explanation of the verdict.
