Yan's closing decision framework starts with the task. Objective requirements generally favor direct checks; subjective preferences often favor pairwise comparisons. Simplifying a valid requirement to a binary decision also makes defect metrics easier to interpret.

Ranking correlations remain useful for ordinal judgments, but they answer a different question. The evaluator's output and the metric should both match the decision the product needs to make.
