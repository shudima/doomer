The structured-output study distinguishes low-level format constraints from higher-level requirements about meaning or style. Participants preferred graphical controls for simple structures and natural language for more complex criteria.

Yan's takeaway is a design distinction. A boolean or JSON shape can be specified mechanically, while a nuanced tone or content requirement may need explanation, examples and a different form of checking.
