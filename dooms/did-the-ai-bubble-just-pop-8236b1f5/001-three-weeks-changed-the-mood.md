Three weeks before this Code Report, AI spending looked unstoppable: SpaceX rallied, tech bosses wanted another $100 billion, and Jensen Huang called chip demand insatiable. Then the mood flipped.

Fireship points to Meta creating non-voting shares to fund GPUs and Microsoft offering a quarter of its employees money to leave. The chip bill, apparently, still comes due.
