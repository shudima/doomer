Fireship sketches a circular financing loop. Nvidia invests in a company that buys Nvidia GPUs. The company borrows the remaining money against those chips, with Nvidia offering to rent otherwise unused capacity.

The investment becomes chip revenue; the chips can then be rented back to their maker. Rising valuations help finance another round. The same company appears on several sides of the deal.
