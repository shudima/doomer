**Illustrative example**

A fictional compute provider struggles to refinance debt while researchers improve model quality. Both developments can happen at once.

Keep two questions separate: can the business fund its commitments, and can the technology perform useful work? Evidence about one does not automatically settle the other. This scenario explains the distinction; it is not a forecast about a real company.
