**Illustrative example**

A hypothetical API price falls. For a developer, that may make an experiment affordable. It does not reveal the provider’s margins or prove the service can sustain that price.

Measure the cost of a successfully completed task and consider the service dependency separately. A lower unit price is useful information, but it answers only part of the business question.
