## Overall summary

Fireship questions the durability of AI infrastructure financing after a market reversal. The report traces Nvidia-linked investment, GPU purchases, collateral and rental arrangements; discusses CoreWeave borrowing and Musk’s neocloud forecast; and reviews financial and safety risks attributed to Anthropic’s IPO filing. It distinguishes fragile financing from technical progress, then argues that developers benefit from subsidized tokens and competitive models. The closing advertisement describes Hyperagent’s shared agent workflow.

The ambiguous reference to a government ban in the introduction was omitted; no explanatory fact was invented. No external reporting, filing, model benchmark, mathematical proof, product plan eligibility or quoted individual’s risk estimate was independently verified.
