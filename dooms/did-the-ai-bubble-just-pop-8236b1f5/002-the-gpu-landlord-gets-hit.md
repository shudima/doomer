A neocloud is a company built around renting out AI computing capacity. Fireship uses CoreWeave as the cautionary example: a former Ethereum miner that pivoted into Nvidia GPU rentals, including rentals back to Nvidia.

The report says CoreWeave was worth only a third of its value at the start of October. Selling access to powerful chips does not make the landlord invincible.
