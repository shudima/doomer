For developers, Fireship sees a brighter immediate picture: AI providers are competing to sell subsidized tokens. The report points to Kimi’s open model, described as within a point of Opus at half the price.

A shaky business for the supplier can still mean useful tools for the customer. The closing joke has CoreWeave’s financed GPUs heading to eBay; it is a punchline, not a liquidation announcement.
