Delegation gives someone responsibility for an outcome. That includes room to make choices you would not make.

Agree on the goal, constraints and check-in points. Then let the person solve the problem. Yan warns that taking work back whenever the approach differs prevents people from developing judgment. Step in when the outcome or an important boundary is at risk, rather than to enforce your preferred style.
