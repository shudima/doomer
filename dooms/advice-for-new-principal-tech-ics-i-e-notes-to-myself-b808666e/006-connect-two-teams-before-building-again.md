**Illustrative example**

Two teams need the same customer lookup. One already has a reliable service; the other is about to build a second version.

Introduce the owners, agree on the missing capability and help them adopt the existing service. Your useful output may be the agreement and a small interface change. Count the duplicated work avoided, rather than looking only at code you personally wrote.
