A principal can belong to many teams without feeling fully part of any. Yan recommends peers with whom candid conversations are possible, even outside the same company or domain.

He also argues for work that supports learning and well-being. Sustained growth is better for both the person and organization than treating every personal need as selfish until burnout forces a reckoning.
