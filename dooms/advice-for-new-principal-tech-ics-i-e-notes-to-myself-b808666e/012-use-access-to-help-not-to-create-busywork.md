A principal's relationships and context can make a small intervention unusually valuable. The same credibility can also turn an offhand remark into unnecessary work for someone else.

Yan recommends acting on high-value opportunities while clearly distinguishing requests, tentative observations and areas outside your expertise. The authority attached to a title makes careless ambiguity more expensive for the organization.
