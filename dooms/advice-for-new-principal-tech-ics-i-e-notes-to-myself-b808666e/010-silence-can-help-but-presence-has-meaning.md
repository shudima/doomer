If a team is reasoning well, a principal need not add comments merely to demonstrate value. Executive meetings can also succeed without exhausting an agenda when they resolve the important decisions.

However, silent attendance may imply approval. Yan includes a warning to stay attentive to what your presence communicates instead of multitasking through a discussion that others believe you have endorsed.
