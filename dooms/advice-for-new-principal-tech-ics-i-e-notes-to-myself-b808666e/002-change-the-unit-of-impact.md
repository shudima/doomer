Your impact can grow without writing more code yourself. At principal level, useful work often changes what several teams can accomplish.

Help them agree on a direction, remove a shared obstacle, or build a capability they can reuse. Eugene Yan describes this as a change in the unit of impact: judge the outcome across the organization, rather than counting only your own implementations.
