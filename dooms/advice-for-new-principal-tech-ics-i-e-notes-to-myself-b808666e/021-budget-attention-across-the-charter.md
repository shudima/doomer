Yan illustrates his charter with one or two owned projects taking more than half his time, two or three sponsored projects taking roughly a fifth, and consulting in the remaining time.

These are his operating examples, not required ratios. Their value is making commitments explicit enough to discuss whether the principal's limited attention matches the organization's actual priorities.
