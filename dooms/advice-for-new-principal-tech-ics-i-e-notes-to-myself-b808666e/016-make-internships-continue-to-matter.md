A few timely interactions with an intern and mentor can materially improve the experience. Early feedback can change direction before too much work accumulates, while demo-day attention helps recognize and connect the work.

Yan also recommends deliverables that survive the internship: working software, product explanations and technical documentation that someone else can extend instead of abandoning the project when the placement ends.
