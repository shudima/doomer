People promoted to principal have usually been demonstrating the role already. Yan recommends continuing those behaviors while developing a style and agreeing on areas of focus.

The autonomy that follows is paired with accountability. The expectation is not to await assignments indefinitely, but to identify important problems and take responsibility for choosing where technical effort can have the most impact.
