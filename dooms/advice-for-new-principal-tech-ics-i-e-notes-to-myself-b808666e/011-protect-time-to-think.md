Reviews, escalations and requests can fill every hour of a broad technical role. Becoming the organization’s default attendee can leave no room for the work that most needs your judgment.

Yan recommends pushing back, delegating and scheduling real thinking time. If you cannot explain why your current work requires a principal, reconsider whether your attention is going to the right problems.
