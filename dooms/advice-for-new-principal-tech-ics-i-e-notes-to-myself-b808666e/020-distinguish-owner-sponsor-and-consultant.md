Be clear about the role you are taking.

- An **owner** is responsible for the outcome.
- A **sponsor** helps secure support and remove obstacles.
- A **consultant** offers advice.

Yan warns against drifting between these roles without saying so. People need to know whether you are deciding, helping them decide, or backing their decision. Clear expectations prevent both accidental takeover and missing ownership.
