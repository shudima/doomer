Personal output has a ceiling. Coaching and mentoring can make a larger part of the organization capable of making good decisions without constant principal involvement.

Yan recommends deliberate investment in a small number of developing technical leaders. Give them meaningful ownership, set them up to succeed and let them build the capabilities that previously made you indispensable.
