Sometimes the valuable intervention is a connection rather than an implementation. One team may already have solved a problem another team is about to tackle.

Yan also includes connecting an opportunity with someone who can grow by owning it. A principal's broad view becomes useful when it reduces duplicated effort and helps the right people find relevant experience or responsibility.
