The scorecard has twelve dimensions and four levels: unaddressed, informal, engineered, then measured and continually improved.

Dimensions cover design, inputs, loop, tools, context, **retrieval**, identity, security, verification, presentation, operations and release. Checkpoints or permission filters establish engineered mechanisms. Recovery measurements, boundary tests and production outcomes show whether those mechanisms keep working. Implementation alone does not establish the highest maturity level.
