Match the grader to the requirement. Code can check exact values, saved state, schemas and action constraints. A model judge can assess qualities that need interpretation.

Give each judge a narrow, observable criterion and compare its decisions with human labels. A different model family may reduce shared blind spots, but it still needs validation. Keep human review for calibration and subjective cases. Trust the score only as far as its grading process earns.
