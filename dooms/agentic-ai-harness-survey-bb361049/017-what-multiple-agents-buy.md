Fresh contexts and parallel reading are delegation's main benefits. The survey cites Anthropic's internal research evaluation: roughly 90 percent better performance on breadth-oriented tasks, using about fifteen times the tokens of chat.

Token use explained about 80 percent of performance variance in that analysis. These vendor-specific results support measuring the quality gain and cost multiplier on your own tasks.
