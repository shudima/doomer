An approval request should identify exactly what will happen, to whom and why, with the relevant evidence attached. Let the person edit the proposal or approve only part of a related batch.

Reversibility and cancellation windows can reduce the cost of mistakes. Excessive requests create fatigue, so monitor both approval rates and decision times. Near-instant approval of almost everything may mean a gate is unnecessary or has become a ritual nobody reads.
