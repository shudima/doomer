pass@k measures the chance of at least one success across k attempts. pass^k measures the chance that all k attempts succeed. They answer very different questions.

For an agent people depend on, repeated success matters more than finding one lucky run. The survey cites tau-bench results with success across eight runs below 25 percent in one domain. Repeat each task and report confidence intervals, so small datasets and random variation do not masquerade as improvements.
