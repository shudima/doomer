Multi-step work needs visible progress so users can understand the direction and interrupt it. Stream useful status, such as reading profiles or checking availability, without dumping raw tool output.

Present lists, comparisons and decisions in structured components. Link claims to evidence, explain definitions behind numbers and expose missing data or failed **verification**. Keep completed actions distinguishable from proposals. The interface should make both the result and its limitations easy to assess.
