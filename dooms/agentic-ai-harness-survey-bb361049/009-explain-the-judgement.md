Good instructions describe goals, success, trade-offs and reasons. A few representative examples can guide unfamiliar cases better than endless exceptions.

Keep repository instruction files short, pointing to deeper material. Put specialised procedures in skills and exact requirements in code. Read the whole prompt for contradictions and accumulated patches that conceal missing tools or controls. Instructions should support judgement at the right level.
