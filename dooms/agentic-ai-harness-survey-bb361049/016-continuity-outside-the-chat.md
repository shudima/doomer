Work spanning many context windows needs durable artefacts. The survey's coding-agent example begins with setup, explicit requirements and a progress file.

Later sessions read those records and version history, complete one item, verify it and update the notes. Continuity survives without an ever-growing conversation. External records preserve what is done, what remains and how completion was checked.
