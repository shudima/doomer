A model can choose an action. The surrounding software makes that action useful.

That software is the **harness**: tools, context, saved state, permissions, budgets and checks. It also decides how to show results to the user.

The survey argues that these pieces determine much of an agent’s practical quality. A stronger model still needs the right information, usable tools and feedback when something goes wrong.
