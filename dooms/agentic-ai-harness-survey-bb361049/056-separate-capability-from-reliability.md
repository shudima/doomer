**Illustrative example**

An agent succeeds on one task in one of five attempts. That shows a possible solution, but a user who tries once usually sees a failure.

Keep the task fixed and record every attempt. Report one success out of five rather than only the best transcript. Then investigate the four failures before deciding whether the system is ready for unattended use.
