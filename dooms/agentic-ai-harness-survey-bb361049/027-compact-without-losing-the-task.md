Light compaction removes material that can be fetched again. Heavy compaction summarises history, so details may disappear or drift.

A useful summary preserves the goal, constraints, decisions and reasons, open questions and references to external records. The survey mentions common thresholds around 70 to 85 percent of the window, but recommends measuring the right point. Keep useful failure evidence and the current plan visible, then test whether early constraints survive long sessions and compaction.
