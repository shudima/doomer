A precise headline can hide a flawed evaluation. Inspect partial runs, changing denominators, judge parsing errors and correct refusals labelled as failures.

Check dataset selection, agreement with human graders and consistent populations across repeated runs. Read transcripts alongside scores to separate agent failures from grader defects. Confidence depends on whether the team can explain how its measurements were produced.
