A quarantined model can read untrusted material without tools while a privileged planner stays separate. The survey also describes CaMeL: a trusted-request-derived program tracks untrusted values and checks data flows before calls.

These approaches constrain flexibility when actions depend on untrusted content. Narrow recipients, restricted exports and approval of exact outgoing text can also block specific harmful paths.
