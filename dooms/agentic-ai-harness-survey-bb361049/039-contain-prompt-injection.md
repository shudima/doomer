Instructions hidden in documents, emails or tool results can redirect an agent. The survey treats model defences as helpful but insufficient guarantees.

Its Rule of Two avoids combining untrusted input, sensitive access and external communication or state changes in one unrestricted session. When all three are needed, separate capabilities or require concrete approval at the dangerous boundary. Contain the consequences of manipulation.
