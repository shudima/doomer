Human involvement can mean approving, editing, answering questions or reviewing **reversible** work afterward. Choose the interaction from the action's risk.

The survey proposes free reads, notification for reversible writes and approval for consequential external or irreversible actions; some capabilities remain unavailable. Policy triggers these gates in code. Durable interrupts preserve state while a person decides, even if the wait lasts hours.
