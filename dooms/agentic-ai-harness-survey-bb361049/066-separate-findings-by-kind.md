Defects are demonstrated failures with reproductions. Structural risks are design choices likely to fail under foreseeable conditions. Measurement gaps mean performance cannot yet be known reliably.

Prioritise by risk and dependency: observability and authorisation, then security containment, evaluation, and evidence-guided quality improvements. This prevents an attractive enhancement from displacing a boundary failure that undermines the system.
