**Illustrative example**

Two teams ask for “active customers.” One means customers who signed in this week; the other means customers with a paid subscription.

A correct SQL query can still answer the wrong question. Store the approved definition with the metric, including its time window and exclusions. The agent can retrieve that definition before assembling the query.
