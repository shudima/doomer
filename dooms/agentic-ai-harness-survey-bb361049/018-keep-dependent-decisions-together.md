Parallel reading and shared decision-making have different needs. Eight workers might assess ten applicants each, while one parent ranks all 80 using the same requirements.

Give each worker a bounded objective, evidence requirements and a clear output format. Keep decisions that must agree in one context. Handoffs suit distinct domains; separate critics suit review. Every delegated agent should inherit the user's permissions, and each extra boundary should justify its coordination cost.
