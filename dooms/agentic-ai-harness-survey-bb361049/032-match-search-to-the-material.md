An agent can search, inspect results and refine its query instead of accepting one **retrieval** before answering. That flexibility helps multi-part questions, at the cost of calls and latency.

Exact file tools often suit code and logs. Graph retrieval may help connect relationships across documents, with significant indexing cost. Loading a whole corpus can work when it is small and stable. Choose the method from the content and task, and bound iterative search with a step budget.
