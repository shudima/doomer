A product combines prompts, **retrieval**, model choices and generation settings. Changing any of them can improve one behavior while damaging another.

Yan recommends representative evaluations so these changes can be measured repeatedly. Without them, teams either inspect outputs manually after every adjustment or operate without dependable evidence that the product is improving.
