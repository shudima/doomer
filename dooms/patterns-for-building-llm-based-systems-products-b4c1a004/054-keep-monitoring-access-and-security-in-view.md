Yan emphasizes measuring real product outcomes after deployment, including experiments that may show an AI feature is not worth keeping. A plausible demo does not establish business value.

The article's practitioner additions also raise access controls, security, consistent formats, tool use and task decomposition. These broaden the system perspective beyond the generator and remind builders that production responsibilities extend to every connected component.
