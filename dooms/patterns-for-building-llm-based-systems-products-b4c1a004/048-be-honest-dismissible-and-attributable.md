Yan recommends stating realistic limits, letting people ignore unwanted assistance and showing where information or recommendations came from. Citations and context from actual human sources can help people assess an answer.

These choices may reduce immediate engagement or confidence, but can improve long-term trust. The aim is useful, calibrated reliance rather than making every model output appear equally authoritative.
