The Microsoft guidelines reviewed by Yan organize assistance around stages of interaction: first impressions, normal use, mistakes and long-term behavior. They emphasize clear capability expectations and efficient correction or dismissal.

This frames interface design around the user's journey. A system should help people understand and recover from its limits, instead of expecting them to infer those limits through repeated frustration.
