A chat box offers flexibility, but also requires users to formulate requests and discover what they can control. Yan questions making it the default for experiences that familiar constrained interactions handle with less effort.

User effort also raises expectations. A click, structured option or recommendation slate may serve the task better, while chat remains available where its flexibility genuinely adds value.
