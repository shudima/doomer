T5 represents varied tasks through text inputs and outputs, allowing a shared interface rather than a different classifier head for every task. InstructGPT combines demonstrations, preference comparisons and reinforcement learning to shape responses.

Yan uses these examples to explain the shift from predicting text toward responding to user instructions. Reusing pretrained knowledge and aligning behavior require additional design beyond the original language-model objective.
