Yan distinguishes continued pretraining, instruction tuning, narrow task tuning and reinforcement learning from preference feedback. These approaches use different data and optimize different behavior.

That distinction prevents confusion about the intended intervention. Teaching a model to follow a response format, adapting it to a domain and training a specialized classifier are related ideas, but they are not interchangeable projects.
