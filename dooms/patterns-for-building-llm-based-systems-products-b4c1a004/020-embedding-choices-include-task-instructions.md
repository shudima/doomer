Yan surveys lightweight word embeddings, sentence encoders and instruction-oriented embedding models. Some distinguish passages from queries through prefixes, while others accept a description of the intended embedding task.

The named models and leaderboard positions are historical examples. The durable principle is to compare representations on the intended workload, including operational requirements, rather than assuming the most convenient API or public ranking is universally best.
