A click or a repeat visit is a clue about usefulness. It is not a complete explanation of what the user wanted.

Someone may click to correct a bad answer, repeat a task because it failed, or leave after getting exactly what they needed. Interpret behavior alongside explicit feedback and the task’s outcome. Otherwise, optimizing engagement can reward the wrong result.
