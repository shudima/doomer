Learning rate, batch size, epochs and sequence length can change adaptation outcomes. Yan also mentions low-rank settings, loss choice and the composition of training examples.

These decisions belong in the measured experiment process. A model's reputation or a fashionable fine-tuning method does not replace checking whether the trained component improves the particular system and preserves its important constraints.
