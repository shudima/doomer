Yan traces the progression from reusable pretrained representations toward task-specific adaptation. ULMFiT demonstrates a language-model adaptation workflow, while BERT and early GPT illustrate different pretraining objectives and architectures.

The shared idea is carrying useful learned knowledge into a smaller supervised task. The task-specific interface and training procedure still need to match what the model is expected to produce.
