**Illustrative example**

An organization has thousands of tools, but an incident investigation needs only search, logs and incident details.

Let the agent discover the relevant tools and load their definitions. Keep access checks on execution even after discovery. A small working catalog reduces confusion without treating discoverability as permission to use everything.
