The video borrows Daniel Kahneman’s distinction between fast, automatic System 1 thinking and slower, deliberate System 2 thinking. Recognizing that two times two is four feels immediate; multiplying 17 by 24 takes more work.

Jev is presented as a System 1 model for quick judgments. Generative reasoning models are the comparison for slower, stepwise work. This is an analogy about their roles.
