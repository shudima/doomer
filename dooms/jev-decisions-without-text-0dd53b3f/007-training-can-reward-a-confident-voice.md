Language-model pre-training learns next-token prediction from large amounts of text. Post-training then shapes how the model responds. In reinforcement learning from human feedback, people’s preferences help train a reward model.

The video highlights a possible side effect: people often prefer confident-sounding answers, so a chatbot can learn to sound sure even when it is wrong. Persuasive delivery and calibrated confidence are different goals.
