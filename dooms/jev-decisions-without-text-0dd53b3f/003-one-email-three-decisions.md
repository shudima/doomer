A customer says they were charged twice and asks for a fix. Before writing a reply, support software needs several judgments: is this a refund request, which team should handle it, and how urgent is it?

These are bounded decisions about the same situation. The video uses them to show where a fast decision model can fit inside an otherwise ordinary application.
