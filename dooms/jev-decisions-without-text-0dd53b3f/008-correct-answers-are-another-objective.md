Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards uses checks such as whether a math answer is right or generated code passes its tests. The presenter connects that approach with reasoning models’ progress in mathematics and coding.

A final-answer reward does not, by itself, reward accurate uncertainty estimates. Working through a longer reasoning process can also add time and expense when the application only needs a quick classification.
