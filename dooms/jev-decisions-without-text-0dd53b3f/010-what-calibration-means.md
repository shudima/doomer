**Calibration** means that a probability matches how often the event happens across comparable predictions.

If a system assigns 80% confidence to many decisions, roughly 80% of that group should be correct. One confident answer cannot establish calibration. The discussion connects this measurable behavior to training the probability itself, rather than rewarding a confident writing style.
