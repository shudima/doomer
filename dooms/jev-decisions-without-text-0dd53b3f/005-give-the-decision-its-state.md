Jev takes the situation being judged, called its state, together with the questions to answer. For the support example, state can include the email and the customer’s recent charges.

The questions have different shapes: a yes/no refund judgment, a choice among billing, technical support and sales, and an urgency score on an ordered scale. All three go into one request.
