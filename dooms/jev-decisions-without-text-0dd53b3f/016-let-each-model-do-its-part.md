A support workflow can use Jev to identify a refund request, an LLM to write the customer’s reply, and Jev again to classify the next incoming response.

The video’s proposal is cooperation between quick decisions and slower generative work. There is no need to make every stage produce prose, or to discard LLMs because a specialized model handles one stage efficiently.
