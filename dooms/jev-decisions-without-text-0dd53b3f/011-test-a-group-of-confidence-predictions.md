**Illustrative example**

A toy system makes one hundred comparable predictions at 80% confidence. If about eighty are right, that group is consistent with calibration.

If only fifty are right, the confidence is too high for that group. One correct prediction cannot settle the question. Use enough representative cases and examine calibration where decisions will actually be made.
