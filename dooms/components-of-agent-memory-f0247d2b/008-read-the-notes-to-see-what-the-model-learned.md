The models left different kinds of notes on the database task. Sonnet 4.6 wrote untested guesses. Opus 4.7 organized its notes and separated confirmed facts from open questions. Sonnet 5 marked answers after grading and recorded corrections.

Opus 5 often saved a rule, the evidence for it, and traps to avoid. Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 also captured validated rules. The files reveal how each model used feedback.
