A note written during the task can preserve why an event matters and be available immediately. Writing it also interrupts the work. A background pass can do the organizing later, with its output becoming available once the pass finishes.

**Illustrative example.** Record the next step before handing off an active incident. After it closes, review the log for a reusable troubleshooting pattern. The two notes serve different deadlines.
