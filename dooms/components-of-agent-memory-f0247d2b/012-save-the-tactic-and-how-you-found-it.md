After seven failed throws at a Magikarp, Fable 5 varied the timing between menu actions and caught it. It recorded the timing change as a tactic to try when several throws fail.

The note carries a useful observation into later sessions. One successful catch after a timing change gives the next player a lead to test. Its evidence is the sequence that happened in that encounter.
