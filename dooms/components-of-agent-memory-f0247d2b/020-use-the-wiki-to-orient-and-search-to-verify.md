On the Stainless questions, direct search met **53%** of graded criteria. With search and a wiki together, the score rose from **54% to 61%** as the wiki matured. The best wiki-only result was **48%**.

Anthropic also reported about **20,000 fewer tokens per question** with both tools. The wiki gives search a head start. The source files can still supply details the wiki missed. Building and maintaining the wiki takes its own compute.
