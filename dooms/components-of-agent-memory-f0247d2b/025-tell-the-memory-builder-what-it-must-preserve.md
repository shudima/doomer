The right structure depends on the job ahead. A personal assistant can organize notes around the person it helps. A wiki for an acquisition brief has a different priority. The wording of contract terms may determine whether the brief is correct.

Anthropic’s first wiki instructions were general. Agents summarized terms into one shared table, and concurrent writes erased some entries. Reviewing the failed brief showed what the memory builder needed to preserve.
