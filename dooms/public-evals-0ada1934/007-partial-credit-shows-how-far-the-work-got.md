A partial score awards credit for the requirements that were met, often with different weights. It can show meaningful progress hidden by a binary result.

Agents’ Last Exam, Finance Agent, and OSWorld report partial and full-pass measures. Reading both helps answer two questions. How much useful work did the agent complete, and how often did it deliver everything the task required?
