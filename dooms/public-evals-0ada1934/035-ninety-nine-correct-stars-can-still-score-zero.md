One science task requires the class and period of all 100 stars to be correct. The article cites a run that got 99 right and received zero under all-pass grading.

Reporting partial credit alongside completion would show **0.99** for that example. The all-pass score still records that the complete requirement was missed. Keeping both makes the distance from full success visible.
