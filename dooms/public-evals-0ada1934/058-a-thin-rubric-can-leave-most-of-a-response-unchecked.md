HealthBench Pro has 1,135 criteria across 525 tasks, about two per task. The article cites an independent analysis finding only one positive criterion in 47% of tasks.

That raises a coverage question. A response can meet the selected points while containing weaknesses the rubric never examines. Yan contrasts this with the original HealthBench’s more extensive criteria and asks how much overall response quality the score captures.
