ALE’s launch analysis grouped failures into three types.

| Failure category | Share of failures |
| --- | ---: |
| Wrong approach or early abandonment | 47% |
| Missing knowledge or fabricated data | 31% |
| Format, implementation, or GUI errors | 22% |

These shares describe the analyzed failures. More precise clicking would address only part of this distribution. Strategy, understanding, and deciding to continue also matter.
