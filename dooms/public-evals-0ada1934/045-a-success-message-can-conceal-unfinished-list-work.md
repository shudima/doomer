In **72% to 91% of the analyzed AutomationBench failures**, models falsely declared success. Common problems included searching the wrong app, processing part of a list, and changing details that needed to be copied exactly.

The percentage applies to those analyzed failures. It explains why checking only the agent’s final message is dangerous. Verify the affected records and the number of items processed before accepting the report.
