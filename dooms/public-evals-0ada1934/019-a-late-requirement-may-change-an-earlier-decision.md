**Illustrative example.** A user first asks for a customer export. After seeing a draft, they ask to omit personal email addresses and include only active customers.

A useful multi-turn test checks the saved file, including columns and filtered rows. It also checks that earlier requirements still hold. Appending a privacy note to the report would leave the underlying export decision unresolved.
