Artificial Analysis reported material hallucinations in **more than 60%** of deliverables that passed every criterion in its LAB evaluation. Examples included incorrect parties, amounts, and dates.

That result belongs to its evaluation setup, which differs from Harvey’s and Vals AI’s. It still illustrates a grading gap. Confirming expected points can leave unsupported claims elsewhere in the document undetected. Source-based review needs to cover those claims too.
