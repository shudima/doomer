Public evaluations provide demanding tasks and increasingly careful infrastructure. Use them to narrow the models worth testing.

Then inspect the grading rules, repeated-run reliability, error analysis, and human work left behind. Run representative tasks through your own harness and check the outputs you depend on. That connects the public result to the actual work your users need finished.
