Finance Agent uses a three-model jury to assess severity-weighted rubric checks. Failing any designated **dealbreaker** sends the task score to zero. Full-pass rate is a secondary measure.

At the v2.1 launch, the leading private-test result was 68% partial credit and 59% all-pass. Those two numbers answer different questions about the same submitted work. Inspect which requirements were made dealbreakers before interpreting either.
