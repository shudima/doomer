Benchmark authors often select tasks that current models struggle with. BrowseComp filtered questions against strong browsing models. AutomationBench added distractions and conflicting policies to easy workflows.

This preserves room to measure future improvements. It also means the resulting task mix differs from routine work. A low score on a deliberately difficult set does not directly estimate success across your everyday requests.
