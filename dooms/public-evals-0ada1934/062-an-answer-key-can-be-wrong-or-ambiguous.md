In BrowseComp’s human study, people who solved a question disagreed with the reference on **50 of 367 questions**, about 14%. Disagreement alone does not prove the reference is wrong.

A later paper confirmed errors in **10 of 200** sampled BrowseComp-Plus questions, which reuse the original questions. Separate confirmed defects from unresolved ambiguity when reviewing the score. A single reference assumes more certainty than some questions support.
