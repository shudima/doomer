OSWorld’s analysis found failures from lost requirements, overlooked messages, guessing instead of asking, and submission without checking. Early details could disappear before the final deliverable.

The paper reports that basic clicking and coding were less often the problem. Preserve the requirements in working state and verify the final result against them. Improving interface control alone would leave these longer-horizon failures unresolved.
