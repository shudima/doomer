A benchmark can change when maintainers repair a test, remove a task, or add harder work. Agents’ Last Exam v1.1 retired 30 tasks and revised 57. Harvey LAB’s public set grew substantially after launch.

Record the exact release beside a score. Two percentages from different versions may describe different exams, even when the benchmark name stays the same.
