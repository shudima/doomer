The article cites separate reviews of Terminal Bench 4.0. Epoch AI reported scoring defects in **30 of 66 tasks**, including 11 that failed correct solutions. Preference Model found unstated requirements in **41 of 65 rebuilt tasks**.

After making requirements explicit and closing shortcuts, one model’s score moved from 63% to 89%. Several things changed together, so the result describes that rebuilt evaluation.
