OSWorld graders inspect the final state against a rubric averaging 27 criteria. Most checks are code-based. Narrow model judgments cover some visual requirements and account for 11.5% of the total score.

At launch, the leading agent completed **20.6%** of tasks while scoring **54.8%** on partial credit. The gap shows why both measures matter. Substantial progress can still leave an incomplete workflow.
