GDPval’s common failures included missing requirements, broken formatting, and inaccurate data or calculations. Performance also weakened on longer tasks.

In Yan’s sample of twelve public tasks, roughly half the rubric criteria checked instruction following, such as a named section or page limit. The rest checked correctness or judgment. That sample helps explain why a professional-work score can still depend heavily on basic requirement completion.
