Some OSWorld tasks include a simulated user who can answer questions. Others introduce emails or chat messages that change requirements during execution.

This makes clarification and adaptation observable parts of the task. The agent needs to notice new information, connect it to the current plan, and preserve earlier constraints that still apply. Yan highlights this design as closer to how work develops through interaction.
