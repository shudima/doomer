A **false positive** occurs when flawed work passes. A rubric might confirm that each expected point appears while missing an invented date, an incorrect amount, or an extra harmful action.

Review passing outputs against the source material and intended outcome. Checks for required content cover only part of correctness. They also need a way to catch consequential errors elsewhere in the deliverable.
