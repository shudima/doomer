HealthBench Pro’s judge checks rubric items separately. Positive items add points and negative items subtract them. Dividing by the maximum positive total produces a score that can go below zero.

The primary result also adjusts for response length around **2,000 characters**, by 1.47 points per 500 characters. Read both the content criteria and this adjustment to understand what a higher score rewards.
