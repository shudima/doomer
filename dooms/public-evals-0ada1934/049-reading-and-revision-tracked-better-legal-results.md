Harvey’s behavioral analysis associated reading more than 90% of the matter documents before drafting with higher scores. Checking and revising also tracked better results, while drafting without review tracked worse ones.

These are reported associations in the analyzed runs. They suggest concrete behaviors to investigate in your own workflow. Read enough evidence to support the draft, then inspect the resulting artifact for omissions and unsupported claims.
