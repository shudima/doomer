Yan describes a legal task whose required meeting date appeared only in its rubric. Another criterion requested a damages estimate absent from the matter documents. A faithful agent could lack the evidence needed to satisfy either.

Harvey removed 17 unsolvable criteria from the held-out set, according to Vals AI. Under all-pass grading, even one impossible criterion can fail an otherwise sound deliverable.
