Under **all-pass scoring**, every required check must pass for the task to earn credit. This can fit work where one missed obligation makes the deliverable unusable. Harvey LAB applies that reasoning to legal work.

The rule also magnifies grader defects. If one assertion rejects a valid answer, the whole task fails. Audit the individual checks before interpreting a low completion rate.
