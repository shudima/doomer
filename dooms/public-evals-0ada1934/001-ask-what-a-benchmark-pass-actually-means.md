A score depends on the task, the workspace, and the rule used to grade the result. Before trusting a model ranking, look at what those pieces reward.

Eugene Yan reviewed twelve public evaluations. In eight, audits or issue reports identified graders rejecting valid work. Some also accepted flawed outputs. His review uses those gaps to ask what benchmarks tell us about dependable work.
