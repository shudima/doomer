Real requests develop through discussion. A user reviews a draft, adds a constraint, or changes direction. A single complete prompt cannot show how an agent handles that process.

The article cites research reporting lower performance in simulated multi-turn conversations. It also highlights OSWorld tasks with clarification and mid-task messages. Those tasks can expose guessing, forgotten constraints, and failure to rethink earlier choices.
