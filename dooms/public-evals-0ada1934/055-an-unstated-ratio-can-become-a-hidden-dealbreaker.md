A public Finance Agent question asked which of three companies was most leveraged. Its earlier rubric also treated peer averages of three ratios as dealbreakers.

An answer naming the correct company could therefore fail for omitting those averages. The article notes a later rubric update. This example shows why critical criteria need to be visible in the request or clearly supported by the task’s professional conventions.
