An outside review reported checks rejecting valid work in 23 of 108 OSWorld v2.1 tasks. One free-text field had to match a short name even though the task website displayed a longer one.

An agent copying the visible name could lose points and fail the whole task. Compare the prompt, interface, and evaluator when reviewing this kind of error. The agent may have followed the information it was actually given.
