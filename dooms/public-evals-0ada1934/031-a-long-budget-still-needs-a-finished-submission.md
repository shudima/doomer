FrontierSWE found agents stopping far before the 20-hour limit or using the whole budget without submitting. Some optimized narrow cases or tried shortcuts caught by grading.

The supplied self-check tool helps an agent track its score. Yan also points out the trade-off. Real engineering often requires deciding what to measure before improving it. A ready-made score exposes implementation ability while leaving less room to assess that judgment.
