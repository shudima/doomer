To grade a task fairly, authors often spell out exactly what the deliverable must contain. That makes implementation easier to check. It can also remove the need to decide what to build.

Agents’ Last Exam turns some creative work into reproducing a target. FrontierSWE supplies a self-check tool that tracks the score. These choices help verification while narrowing how much independent scoping and measurement judgment the task tests.
