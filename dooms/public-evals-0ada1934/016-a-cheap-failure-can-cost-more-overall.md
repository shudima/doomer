**Illustrative example.** One agent costs $1 and leaves twenty minutes of checking and repair. Another costs $2 and needs two minutes of review.

At an assumed $60 per hour for human time, the totals are **$21** and **$4**. These are toy figures. They show why a lower token bill can be a poor guide when human attention is the expensive part of finishing the task.
