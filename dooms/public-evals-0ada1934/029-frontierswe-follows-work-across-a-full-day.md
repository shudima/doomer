FrontierSWE v2 offers 34 engineering and research tasks with a **20-hour budget**. Models run five trials per task at maximum reasoning effort in Proximus, which adds image viewing, submission, and compaction to mini-swe-agent.

Scores award partial credit between a baseline and a full-credit target. At its September 2026 launch, the top score was 56%. Reported trials often used 100–500 million tokens. The cheapest model above 30% averaged $97 per trial.
