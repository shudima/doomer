GDPval’s expert graders agreed with one another about **71%** of the time. Its automated grader agreed with experts about **66%** of the time.

Those figures describe agreement in the comparison procedure. They give context for judging differences between systems on subjective work. Inspect disagreement cases and the review instructions before treating a small change in win rate as a dependable improvement.
