BrowseComp requests an explanation, a short exact answer, and confidence. A model judge extracts the final answer and compares it with a reference. Whoever runs the evaluation supplies the browsing tools.

At the April 2025 launch, Deep Research answered 51.5% correctly and GPT-4o with browsing 1.9%. These are historical system results on that setup. Tooling and answer-key quality remain part of what the score reflects.
