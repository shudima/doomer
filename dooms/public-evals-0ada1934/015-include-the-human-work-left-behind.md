A model can spend very little by stopping early or skipping review. Cost per attempted task may then make it look efficient while a person has to repair or finish the work.

Yan argues for counting review, debugging, and rework. In GDPval’s analysis, GPT-4o had a much lower model bill than GPT-5, but adding expert review and redo time made GPT-4o slower than an expert working alone. GPT-5 was faster under that analysis.
