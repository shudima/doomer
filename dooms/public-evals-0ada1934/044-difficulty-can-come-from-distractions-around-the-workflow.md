Zapier seeded synthetic AutomationBench tasks with customer workflows that had received negative feedback. Authors hardened easier tasks using irrelevant records, similar names, and conflicting policies.

At the April 2026 launch, the best private-set completion rate was 9.9%. Public results face a different task set and are not directly comparable. Keep the split and its difficulty policy beside any reported score.
