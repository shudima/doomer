A role-based content team might have a researcher gather material, a writer draft an article and an editor revise it. They communicate while remaining within their assigned responsibilities.

The video distinguishes this role-bounded setup from looser autonomous collaboration. It suggests CrewAI, AutoGen with additional structure, and specialized tools such as ChatDev for software work. The boundaries between responsibilities are part of the design.
