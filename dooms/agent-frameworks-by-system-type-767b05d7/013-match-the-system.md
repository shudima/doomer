Before choosing a framework, ask what kind of work you have:

- Known steps: a workflow.
- An open-ended goal: exploratory agents.
- Clear specialist roles: a structured team.
- Business operations: production orchestration.
- An untested idea: a quick visual prototype.

These are the video’s categories. Use them to narrow the comparison, then test the tools against your own task.
