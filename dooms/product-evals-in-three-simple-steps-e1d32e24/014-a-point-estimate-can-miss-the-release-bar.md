Suppose the requirement is a defect rate below 5%. Observing 3% defects across 200 examples does not automatically establish that requirement: Yan's approximate 95% interval extends to about 5.4%.

The uncertainty matters as much as the observed rate. A team should decide whether the evidence supports the release criterion, rather than treating a favorable point estimate as conclusive.
