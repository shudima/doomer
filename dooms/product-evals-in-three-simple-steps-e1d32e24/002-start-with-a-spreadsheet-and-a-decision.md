Collect actual requests, responses and the context needed to judge them. Then label whether each response meets a clearly defined criterion.

Yan recommends pass/fail decisions for objective requirements such as faithfulness. For subjective comparisons, use win/lose/tie judgments. Allowing ties matters: forcing a winner between effectively equivalent responses introduces noise rather than useful preference information.
