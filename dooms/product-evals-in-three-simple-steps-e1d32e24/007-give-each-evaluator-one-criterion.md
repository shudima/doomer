Give each evaluator one clear criterion. Judging faithfulness, relevance, brevity and tone in one prompt makes the verdict hard to interpret.

Separate checks show which dimension failed. Then choose a release rule: an unsupported claim might block shipping, while tone remains a quality target. Yan recommends this separation so the team can improve and calibrate each evaluator.
