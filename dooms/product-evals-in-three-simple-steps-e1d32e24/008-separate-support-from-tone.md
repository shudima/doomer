**Illustrative example**

A support answer is friendly but invents a refund deadline. A tone judge may pass it while a grounding judge fails it.

Keep those checks separate. Use the grounding failure to block the release, then improve the writing without losing that protection. A single average score could let friendliness conceal an unsupported claim.
