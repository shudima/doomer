Once an evaluator is sufficiently precise, it can screen unlabeled examples for likely failures. People then review those candidates instead of labeling thousands of examples without direction.

This is an active-learning approach to building a useful dataset. The automated screening helps allocate annotation effort, while human review continues to establish the labels used to align and assess the evaluator.
