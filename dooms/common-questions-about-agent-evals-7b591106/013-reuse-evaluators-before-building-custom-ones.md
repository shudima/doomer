LangSmith offers evaluator templates for common issues, while the open-source agentevals package checks messages and tool calls. Adapt the prompt or reference trajectory to the behavior your product requires.

A reference-free offline evaluator can also score production runs. The article additionally describes coding-agent skills and LangSmith Engine generating custom checks from traces; those checks still need to express what successful behavior means for your agent.
