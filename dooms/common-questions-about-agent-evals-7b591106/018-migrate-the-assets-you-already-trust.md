Moving an evaluation stack need not mean rebuilding every dataset and judge. Existing examples can be uploaded or created through the SDK, scoring functions can be adapted, and judge prompts can be retained.

Pytest and Vitest integrations can preserve a test-based workflow. The article also emphasizes framework-independent tracing, allowing teams to bring their current agent rather than rewriting it merely to adopt different evaluation tooling.
