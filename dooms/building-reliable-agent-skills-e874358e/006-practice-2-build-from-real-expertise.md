Asking a model to invent a skill can produce generic instructions about validation and error handling. Those instructions may add little knowledge.

The presenters propose two better sources: perform the task once and record what worked, including corrections, or synthesize the procedure from existing reports, runbooks, review comments and pull-request feedback. The useful contribution is the expertise specific to that job.
