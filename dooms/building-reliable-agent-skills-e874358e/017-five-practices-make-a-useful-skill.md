The video closes by joining the five practices: make the skill easy to select, ground it in real expertise, keep its context economical, use deterministic scripts for fragile steps, and review it before execution.

These ideas work together. A procedure must be found, teach something specific, remain usable in context, handle exact operations consistently and respect the access it receives. The presenters expect the practices to keep evolving.
