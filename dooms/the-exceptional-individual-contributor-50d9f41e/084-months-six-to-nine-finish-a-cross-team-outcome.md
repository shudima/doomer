Choose a project that implements part of the strategy across multiple teams. The survey recommends explicit technical ownership, an agreed definition of done tied to quality, a premortem and regular updates.

Incremental rollout helps expose problems while there is time to act. The milestone is a completed outcome recognized by its sponsors, not merely a collection of finished implementation tasks.
