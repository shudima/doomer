In the survey's composite example, a new model scores better overall but regresses on contract-related questions. A targeted adjustment precedes a small traffic rollout with online comparisons and a rollback path.

The lesson is that aggregate improvement does not eliminate category-specific risk. Prompt and model changes deserve a measured release process, followed by cost and quality checks under real use.
