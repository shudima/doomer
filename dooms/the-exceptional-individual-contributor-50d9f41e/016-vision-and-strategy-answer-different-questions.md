A **vision** describes a recognizable future. A **strategy** explains how to reach it from where you are now.

You need both a destination and a workable route. A vision alone can remain an aspiration. A strategy without a destination can keep improving the current setup indefinitely. The survey emphasizes the connection between them over producing impressive documents.
