Exceptional technical work helps other people and systems produce better outcomes. At staff or principal level, your impact extends beyond what you personally implement.

This survey collects practitioner guidance for that transition, especially in AI roles. It is professional advice rather than a controlled study. Its company stories are **illustrative composites**, not reports about particular organizations.
