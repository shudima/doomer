**Scope** is the problem space you are responsible for. **Leverage** is how much impact your limited time can produce through systems and other people.

As a technical role grows, those two ideas matter more than writing the hardest code yourself. Titles vary by company. Agree on the actual responsibility, especially when it spans several teams.
