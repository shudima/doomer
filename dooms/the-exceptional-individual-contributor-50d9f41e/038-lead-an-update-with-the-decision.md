**Illustrative example**

A weak update lists meetings and implementation details. A useful one starts: “The migration is blocked on access approval. We need an owner’s decision by Thursday to keep the launch date.”

Then provide evidence, options and the consequence of waiting. The reader can understand what needs attention before reading the background. This is an added writing example.
