A postmortem matters when its actions are completed. The survey recommends tracking system changes rather than treating the review document itself as the final deliverable.

A principal can also notice patterns across teams: missing dependency records, absent relevance checks or repeated monitoring gaps. Converting those patterns into shared improvements extends incident learning beyond the team that happened to experience the latest failure.
