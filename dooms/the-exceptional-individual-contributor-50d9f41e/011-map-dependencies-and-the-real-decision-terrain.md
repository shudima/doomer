Tanya Reilly's locator map places your team within the organization: its dependencies, customers and business purpose. The topographic map captures how decisions really happen, including informal influence, friction and the history of earlier attempts.

The survey argues that a reporting chart cannot supply all this context. Good proposals can fail when their authors understand the code but misunderstand the organization around it.
