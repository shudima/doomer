**Illustrative example**

“Improve agent quality” is a goal. A strategy might diagnose inconsistent evaluation as the obstacle, choose shared release checks as the policy, and pilot them with one team.

Then define adoption and review steps. The choices support one another, and the diagnosis can be challenged. This added scenario illustrates the strategy kernel rather than prescribing a universal platform.
