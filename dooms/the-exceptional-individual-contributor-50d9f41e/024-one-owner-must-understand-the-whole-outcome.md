The directly responsible individual is accountable for a project's result without personally doing every task. The survey recommends an owner who understands both the technical system and why the work matters.

A written definition of done aligns that owner with sponsors. For AI work, measurable quality criteria are more useful than declaring that an agent appears to work in a demonstration.
