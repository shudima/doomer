In the survey's composite example, an engineer quietly maintains evaluation data for several teams without receiving formal recognition. A principal works with the manager to acknowledge that responsibility.

The engineer then authors a guide credited to them and receives sponsorship to lead a related initiative. The example connects recognition, mentorship and opportunity instead of leaving essential work dependent on an invisible volunteer.
