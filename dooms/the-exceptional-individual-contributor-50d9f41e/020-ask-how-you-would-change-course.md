**Illustrative example**

A team can swap a parser behind a tested interface in a day. Moving a sensitive data store creates migration, access and recovery commitments.

Use a short local experiment for the first decision and deeper review for the second. These are hypothetical conditions: the point is to assess actual reversal cost, not to label all parser or database choices the same way.
