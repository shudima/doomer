In a workflow, code determines the steps and their order. In an agent, the model chooses what to do next within a loop. The survey recommends beginning with a well-designed call and adding structure only when the task requires it.

Predictable work usually favors a workflow. Autonomy can be useful when the necessary steps cannot be known beforehand, but brings more cost and opportunities for compounded errors.
