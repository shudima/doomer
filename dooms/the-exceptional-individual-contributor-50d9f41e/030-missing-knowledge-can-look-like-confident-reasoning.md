In the survey's composite incident, a pipeline stops indexing one document category. **Retrieval** returns poor evidence, and the assistant supplies confident answers from general knowledge instead. Ordinary error logs remain quiet.

The response adds retrieval checks, quality sampling, document freshness metrics and documented dependencies. The example illustrates changing the conditions that permitted failure rather than asking one team simply to be more careful.
