The third map describes where the organization should go and a rough route toward that state. It complements understanding current dependencies and decision-making rather than replacing either.

The survey recommends structured conversations, consistent questions and written learning before major proposals. Candid observations about people may need to remain private. Periodically refreshing the maps prevents an old mental model from quietly becoming misleading.
