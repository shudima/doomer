A brag document records work and its effects so an accurate promotion or impact account can be assembled later. The survey recommends using that record to notice patterns, not only to prepare for review.

Time and energy also require explicit choices. More good work exists than one person can accept, so deciding what not to do is part of maintaining a sustainable and effective senior role.
