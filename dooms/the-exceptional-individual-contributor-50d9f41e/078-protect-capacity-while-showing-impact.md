**Illustrative example**

A principal’s calendar fills with incident help, design reviews and mentoring. None appears in the planned project list.

Record the outcomes and recurring demand, then agree on which work deserves explicit capacity or another owner. Making the work visible supports a deliberate portfolio. The goal is sustainable responsibility, rather than proving value through an ever-longer working day.
