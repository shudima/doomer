Code review provides direct evidence of how a system is changing and a chance to explain trade-offs through specific feedback. In established codebases, the survey favors consistency over imposing a personal style.

Reading production data is equally important. For AI systems, traces show behavior that cannot be inferred from source code alone, including what evidence and tool results the model actually encountered.
