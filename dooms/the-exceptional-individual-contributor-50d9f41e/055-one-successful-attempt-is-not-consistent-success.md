One successful attempt shows that the system can succeed. It does not show that it succeeds reliably.

Run important tasks repeatedly and report the rate of success alongside the conditions. A result such as “passed once in five attempts” tells a different story from “passed all five.” The survey distinguishes capability from consistency because users experience both.
