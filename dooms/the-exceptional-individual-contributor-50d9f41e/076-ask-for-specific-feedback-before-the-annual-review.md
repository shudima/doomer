Senior engineers often receive less frequent, less precise feedback because fewer colleagues can assess the full role. The survey recommends requesting observations about particular decisions or interactions rather than waiting for general appraisal.

A peer network inside and outside the organization can supply another perspective. Growth becomes a deliberate practice with repeated feedback, not an assumed consequence of spending another year at the same level.
