New technology introduces unfamiliar operational costs and failure modes. The survey uses the idea of limited innovation tokens to recommend spending novelty on a genuine advantage while relying on familiar tools elsewhere.

This is not a rule against change. It is a request to understand what the proposed technology solves locally and whether that benefit justifies the additional complexity the team must operate.
