The survey presents Will Larson’s four staff-plus archetypes:

- **Tech Lead:** coordinates a team’s technical work.
- **Architect:** guides a technical domain.
- **Solver:** takes on a difficult problem.
- **Right Hand:** extends a senior leader’s reach.

These describe ways to contribute. One person can combine them or switch as the organization’s needs change.
