Quality is not the only constraint on an AI feature. The survey recommends tracking cost and **latency** deliberately, using measured routing to smaller models, appropriate caching and per-request budgets where they help.

These are system trade-offs to test rather than automatic optimizations. A cheaper path is valuable when it still meets the required quality and user experience, not simply when it reduces the model bill.
