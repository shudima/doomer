Doing every coordinating task personally can make a principal indispensable in an unhealthy way. Avoiding all such work leaves teams unsupported. The survey recommends a deliberate middle path that builds capability elsewhere.

Ask which people have grown through your support and what opportunities you created for them. Effective multiplication includes releasing visible work you could have kept while remaining available as a thoughtful adviser.
