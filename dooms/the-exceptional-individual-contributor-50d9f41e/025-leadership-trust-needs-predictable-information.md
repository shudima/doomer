Project sponsors depend on the lead's estimates and judgment. The survey recommends regular, honest updates that surface delays early and pair problems with a plan.

The lead also needs time available for emerging issues, especially near launch. Owning every implementation task can leave the person responsible for the whole result unable to respond when a cross-team dependency or unexpected risk threatens delivery.
