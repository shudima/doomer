Comfortable work can fill your calendar while the important problem stays untouched. The survey names three traps:

- **Snacking:** easy, satisfying work with little impact.
- **Preening:** visible activity that changes little.
- **Chasing ghosts:** applying your previous employer’s diagnosis before understanding this organization.

Check whether your portfolio addresses a real local need at the scope of your role.
