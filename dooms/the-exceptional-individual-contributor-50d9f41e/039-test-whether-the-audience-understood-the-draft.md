Conversation is immediate but leaves a fragile record. An exhaustive document can fail differently by becoming too long for the intended audience to finish.

The survey recommends writing concise, reader-specific documents on a predictable cadence. Ask someone from the target audience to explain what a draft says; the gap between their interpretation and the intended message identifies where revision is actually needed.
