**Mentoring** helps someone learn. **Sponsorship** helps them reach an opportunity.

A mentor might explain how to lead a design review. A sponsor might recommend the engineer to lead one and help secure support. The survey treats both as ways to multiply impact. Advice alone may not remove the access barriers that keep a capable person from getting meaningful responsibility.
