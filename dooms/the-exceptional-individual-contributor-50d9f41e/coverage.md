## Overall summary

The survey connects principal-level scope and leverage with deliberate work selection, organizational understanding, written strategy and decisions, delivery, reliability, influence, communication, development of others and technical judgment. Its AI layer emphasizes trace-based error analysis, evaluation, proportionate autonomy, context management, production controls and disciplined use of coding agents. It finishes with sustainable growth practices and an adaptable twelve-month sequence.

Both summary-table pages (48–49) were checked against the chapter coverage. The contents and bibliography (pages 2 and 50–51) are navigation and provenance rather than separate lessons. Named books and linked essays remain the survey's references; this collection does not claim independent verification of their contents.

Page 51 explicitly identifies all company examples as illustrative composites. Example dooms retain that qualification. Their outcomes and numbers are not described as observed results from real organizations. The repeated-trial arithmetic states the independence assumption. The twelve-month schedule is framed as the survey's adaptable sequence, not a universal timetable.

No substantive source-access or extraction gaps were found. Line-wrap hyphenation and repeated running headers were omitted during paraphrasing. The original table and page layouts remain available in the preserved attachment; the reel collection does not reproduce every row or glossary definition verbatim.
