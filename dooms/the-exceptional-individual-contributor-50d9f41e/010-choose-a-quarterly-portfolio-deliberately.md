**Illustrative example**

Three teams are shipping agents, but none can measure whether model changes help. You could spend the quarter polishing one team’s prompts or proving a shared evaluation approach.

Write the desired outcome, who will use it and how you will know it helped. Review the trade-off with your manager. This added scenario illustrates portfolio choice; it is not a reported company result.
