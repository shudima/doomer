Guardrails can reject, alter or escalate risky inputs, outputs and actions. The survey recommends stronger constraints for more consequential operations, such as changing records or making commitments, than for simple information **retrieval**.

Possible controls include narrow parameters and human approval. The aim is a system whose available actions and safeguards match the consequences of error, rather than relying on fluent model instructions as the entire boundary.
