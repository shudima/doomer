Match the decision process to how hard the choice is to reverse. Cheap experiments can move quickly. Long-lived commitments need more investigation.

The survey uses the idea of one-way and two-way doors. Ask about the real cost of changing course, rather than assuming every technical choice belongs in one category. This helps avoid both endless review and rushed commitments.
