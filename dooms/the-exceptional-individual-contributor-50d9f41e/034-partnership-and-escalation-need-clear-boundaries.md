Senior ICs, engineering managers and product partners have different responsibilities. The survey recommends agreeing on decision ownership and collaborating rather than competing to control the team.

Escalation can resolve a genuine deadlock, but routinely obtaining mandates can weaken trust. The recommended alternative is evidence, stakeholder understanding and, when escalation is necessary, transparency with the other party about what is happening and why.
