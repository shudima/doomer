In the survey's composite example, a principal prototypes routing simple requests to a smaller model, reads production traces and reviews important changes. They deliberately avoid owning unpredictable sprint commitments.

When the prototype produces useful evidence, a short design proposal supports handoff to another engineer for production implementation. The example keeps technical depth connected to the team's actual system without turning the principal into its delivery bottleneck.
