The survey's plan assumes a technically strong engineer newly entering principal AI **scope**. Its first stage is structured listening, three organizational maps and an explicit agreement with the manager about responsibilities.

It also includes reading production traces and assessing quality, cost and reliability. This establishes a grounded starting point before proposing an organization-wide vision based on incomplete understanding.
