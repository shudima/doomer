The survey recommends reading real traces from beginning to end and noting failures without forcing them into a predefined taxonomy. This is open coding. Grouping those observations into useful categories is axial coding.

Counting the categories helps identify recurring and harmful problems. Building evaluators first can reverse that logic, leaving a team measuring hypothetical defects while missing the failures users actually experience.
