The survey's composite routine combines a brief weekly impact record, monthly requests for specific feedback, quarterly peer conversations and protected learning time. Reading alternates with small experiments and a written account of findings.

The particular schedule is illustrative rather than mandatory. Its purpose is to turn learning into an ongoing **feedback loop** that remains connected to work instead of an endless stream of unread announcements.
