In a composite migration example, a premortem reveals missing security review and a product whose **latency** budget the platform cannot yet meet. Both issues receive attention early.

When one migration needs more time, the principal explains the delay and ships the other products as planned. Measured quality and gradual traffic changes make progress visible without disguising unresolved risk as completed work.
