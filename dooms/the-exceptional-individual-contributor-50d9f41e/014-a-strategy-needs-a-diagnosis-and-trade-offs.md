A useful strategy explains three things:

1. **Diagnosis:** what is happening and why it matters.
2. **Guiding policy:** the approach you will take.
3. **Coherent actions:** concrete steps that support that approach.

The survey uses Richard Rumelt’s strategy kernel. A list of goals or technologies is not enough. Explain the constraints and trade-offs, including what you will deliberately postpone.
