Understanding the organization makes a useful improvement easier. A useful improvement builds credibility for direction-setting. Direction helps coordinate delivery, and delivery creates opportunities to grow others.

The survey recommends this progression while adapting the timing to local needs. Starting immediately with a grand vision, spending the year only implementing or retreating entirely into coordination each misses a different part of the principal role.
