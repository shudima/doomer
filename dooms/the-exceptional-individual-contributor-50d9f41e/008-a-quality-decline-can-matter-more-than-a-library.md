In a composite example, a principal chooses between a prompt library, an internal presentation and unexplained degradation in an agent's success rate. The decline sits between teams, so nobody has taken responsibility.

Investigating reveals stale **retrieval** data and a confusing tool description. The principal fixes those causes and adds checks, while handing the library idea to another engineer as a development opportunity.
