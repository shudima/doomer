In the survey's composite example, a team considers a multi-agent framework for a fixed sequence of model calls. The principal asks which current problem requires the framework's flexibility.

The team keeps the simpler workflow and improves its weakest steps through evaluation. The lesson is conditional: reconsider added orchestration when a concrete need appears, rather than treating flexibility as a benefit independent of cost.
