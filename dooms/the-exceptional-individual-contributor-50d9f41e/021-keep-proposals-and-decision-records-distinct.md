A proposal helps people make a decision. A decision record explains the choice after it is made.

| Document | What it preserves |
| --- | --- |
| Design proposal or RFC | Problem, alternatives, goals and risks |
| Architecture decision record | Chosen direction, context and consequences |

Keep them proportional to the decision. Future engineers need the reasoning, not another debate with the original context missing.
