The final stage develops other engineers, sponsors someone for a visible opportunity and transfers ownership so the work no longer depends on its original principal.

Review the impact record, obtain feedback and revisit **scope** with the manager. Update the strategy using what actually happened. This completes a cycle of learning and organizational capability rather than leaving the year as a set of personally owned projects.
