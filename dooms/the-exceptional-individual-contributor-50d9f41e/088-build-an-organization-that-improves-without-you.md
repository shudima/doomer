Build an organization that can keep improving without depending on you for every decision.

The survey’s closing advice connects technical depth with shared systems, clear ownership and growing other people. Leave behind working evaluation, useful context, reliable delivery and people who can make good choices. Your own output still matters; the larger goal is to make the surrounding organization more capable.
