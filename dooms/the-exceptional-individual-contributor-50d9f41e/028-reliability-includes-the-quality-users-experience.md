A service-level objective defines a measurable reliability target; its error budget is the amount of failure that target allows. The survey uses this to connect release risk with an explicit reliability policy.

AI systems also need quality measures. An agent can become less useful without throwing errors, so availability and **latency** alone may miss a serious deterioration in the experience users actually receive.
