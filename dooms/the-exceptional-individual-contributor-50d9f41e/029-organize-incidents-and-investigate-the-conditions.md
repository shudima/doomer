The survey recommends clear incident responsibilities: coordination, communication, investigation and a shared timeline. After recovery, a blameless review asks how the system and process allowed the failure.

Blamelessness does not remove accountability. It directs attention toward changes that prevent recurrence or reduce impact, rather than assuming that identifying an individual mistake is an adequate explanation of the incident.
