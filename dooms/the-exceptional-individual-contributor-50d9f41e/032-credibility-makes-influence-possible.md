Teams outside a principal's reporting line have their own priorities. Technical correctness alone does not oblige them to adopt a proposal.

The survey emphasizes credibility built through delivery and honest uncertainty, relationships established by helping others, and evidence from prototypes or measurements. Framing a proposal around what stakeholders value makes adoption less risky and more relevant to the people being asked to change.
