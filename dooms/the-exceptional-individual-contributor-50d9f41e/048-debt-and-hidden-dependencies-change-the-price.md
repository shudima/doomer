The glossary describes technical debt as a future cost accepted for an expedient choice today; that choice can be sensible when its consequences are understood. Widely used systems also develop dependencies on behavior nobody formally documented.

The survey therefore recommends understanding why an existing design exists before replacing it. A locally cleaner implementation can have a larger migration cost than its author initially sees.
