A correction can leave two incompatible versions in the conversation: the old claim and its replacement. The model may continue using the wrong one.

The video calls this a context conflict. Make the current version clear and remove or explicitly supersede the stale information where possible. Adding another sentence to a growing pile of contradictions may leave the problem unresolved.
