A model’s context limit tells you how much input it can accept. It does not guarantee equal attention to every detail.

The video distinguishes capacity from useful attention. A long history can bury the current task among irrelevant material. Keep the information needed for the next decision prominent, and retrieve supporting detail when it becomes relevant.
