Permanent files can retain detailed information without keeping every line in the model’s active context. The agent can bring the relevant parts back when they are needed.

The video proposes this alongside compaction. A shorter working context does not require throwing away the project’s detailed record; it requires separating what must be actively considered now from what can remain available for later **retrieval**.
