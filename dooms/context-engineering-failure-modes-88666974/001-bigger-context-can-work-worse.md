A **context window** can hold a million tokens without using them all reliably. The presenter cites a study of 18 frontier models whose performance became less reliable as context grew.

The video calls this context rot. Context engineering responds by choosing what enters the window at each step of an agent’s run, rather than assuming a larger container will automatically make a long task easier.
