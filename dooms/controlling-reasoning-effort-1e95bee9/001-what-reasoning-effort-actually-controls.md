A reasoning model generates a problem-solving trace before its final answer. Here, “reasoning” describes output behavior; it does not establish human-like thinking.

**Reasoning effort** controls how much work the model spends on the answer. The mechanism depends on its training: prompt instructions, learned response styles or budget-aware behavior may sit behind the same menu. Raschka asks how one trained model can support these modes without always producing its longest response.
