**Illustrative example**

For a support classifier, compare a small model at higher effort with a larger model at lower effort. Keep the examples and success criteria fixed.

Measure correct classifications, latency and cost for each combination. The settings are experimental choices; a shared “high” label does not establish equal compute or quality across models.
