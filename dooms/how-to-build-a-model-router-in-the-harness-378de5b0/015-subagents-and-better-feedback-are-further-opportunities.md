Open SWE’s subagents currently choose models independently of the router. Applying task-aware selection there could provide additional savings on longer work.

The authors also propose controlled coding benchmarks and richer signals such as user frustration mined from traces. These are possible improvements to a proof of concept, not capabilities established by the original A/B test or evidence that the router is already optimal.
