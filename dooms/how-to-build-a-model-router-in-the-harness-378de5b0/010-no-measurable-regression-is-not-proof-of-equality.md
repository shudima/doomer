An experiment can fail to detect a quality drop without proving that two systems are equal. A small sample or noisy outcome can hide a difference.

Read the size and uncertainty of the reported comparison alongside the cost result. The article’s routing experiment is useful evidence for that workload. It still needs follow-up on the tasks and failures that matter to the deployment.
