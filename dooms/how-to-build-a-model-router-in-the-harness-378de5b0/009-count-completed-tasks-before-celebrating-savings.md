**Illustrative example**

Imagine routing cuts model spend from $100 to $60, but successful tasks fall from 100 to 50. Cost per success rises from $1 to $1.20.

These toy numbers show why a cheaper bill alone can mislead. Define the success measure first and compare both quality and spend under the same task conditions.
