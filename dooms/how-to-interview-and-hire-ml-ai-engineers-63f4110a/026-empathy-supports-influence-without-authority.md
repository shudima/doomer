The article's final trait is genuine interest in customers and colleagues. Yan connects empathy with listening, trust, communication and navigating difficult organizational disagreements.

He and his collaborator present this as an opinion about what makes a strong hire, including their belief that sincerity matters more than learned demonstrations of concern. The recommendation is to examine concrete behavior rather than mistake polished interview technique for care.
