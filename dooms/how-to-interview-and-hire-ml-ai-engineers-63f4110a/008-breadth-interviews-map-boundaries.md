A breadth interview explores relevant areas of machine learning without expecting mastery of every specialty. Yan recommends explaining that probing will eventually reach the edge of the candidate's knowledge.

This makes admitting unfamiliarity acceptable and helps the interviewer spend time productively. Team needs should guide the subjects, rather than turning the interview into a contest against an interviewer who has repeated the same questions many times.
