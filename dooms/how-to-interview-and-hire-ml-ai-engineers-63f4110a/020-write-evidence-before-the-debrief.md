**Illustrative example**

Instead of “strong communicator,” write: “explained two design options, named a constraint for each, and changed the recommendation after a new requirement.”

Connect that observation to the role’s criteria. Other interviewers can compare their evidence with yours. Specific notes make disagreement easier to examine than a collection of confident adjectives.
