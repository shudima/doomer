Treat interviewing as a system you can improve. Decide which abilities the role needs, gather relevant evidence, and compare that evidence consistently.

Eugene Yan separates assessing candidates, running the interview process and attracting people to the opportunity. A strong conversation is useful when it helps you make a well-supported hiring decision, rather than merely confirming an early impression.
