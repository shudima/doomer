**Illustrative example**

A candidate describes a model that looked strong offline but failed after launch. Ask which examples exposed the gap and how the team changed its evaluation.

The answer can reveal data judgment, error analysis and ownership of mistakes. A list of model names would tell you much less about how the candidate works through a misleading result.
