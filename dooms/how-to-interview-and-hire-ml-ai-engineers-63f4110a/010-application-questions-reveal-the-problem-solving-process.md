A practical design problem often has no single correct answer. Yan values the candidate's clarifying questions, trade-offs and ability to connect engineering with product and business goals.

For recommendations involving frequently changing items, relevant issues include sparse data, exploration and competing engagement or revenue objectives. The proposed architecture matters, but so does the reasoning that makes it appropriate to the situation.
