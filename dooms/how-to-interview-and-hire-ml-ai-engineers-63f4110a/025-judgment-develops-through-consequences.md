Judgment helps a person choose plausible paths, avoid expensive dead ends and act under uncertainty. In machine learning, that may mean choosing an approach suited to sparse data instead of pursuing a fashionable but unsuitable solution.

Yan notes that the consequences of important decisions can take many months to emerge. For roles needing mature judgment immediately, a demonstrated history can therefore matter.
