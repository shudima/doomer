Yan and his collaborator value hunger: initiative, fast learning and persistence through obstacles. They see it as useful both when a startup changes direction and when a large organization creates difficult barriers.

Their view is that intrinsic drive is harder to manufacture than technical knowledge. Imposed urgency can create activity, but they do not regard it as an equivalent substitute.
