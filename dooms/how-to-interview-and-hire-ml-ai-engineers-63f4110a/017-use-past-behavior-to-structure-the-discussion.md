The STAR structure organizes an example around situation or task, the candidate's actions and the results. Yan uses it to connect the initial stakes, difficult decisions and measurable outcomes.

Follow-up questions should establish the person's actual responsibility, not merely the project's success. Useful evidence includes how they defined success, navigated constraints and determined whether the final outcome helped customers or the business.
