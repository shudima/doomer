Write your feedback before hearing the other interviewers’ conclusions. Record what the candidate did or said, then connect that evidence to the role’s criteria.

This keeps the debrief from becoming a contest of confidence or a repetition of the first opinion expressed. Yan recommends independent feedback so the group can compare distinct observations and examine disagreements.
