Making someone comfortable is compatible with maintaining a demanding standard. Yan recommends treating the discussion as a collaborative effort to uncover ability rather than an attempt to expose failure.

Small courtesies and a clear explanation of the interview's purpose can reduce anxiety. Even an unsuccessful candidate should leave with a worthwhile experience, rather than feeling treated as a disposable input to the hiring process.
