Machine-learning outputs do not behave like perfectly predictable database lookups. Retraining can change predictions, language-model responses vary and internal reasoning may remain opaque.

Yan suggests asking how candidates handled unexpected or biased outputs and what validators or policies they added around the model. The useful evidence is how they align overall system behavior with user needs despite imperfect control over the model itself.
