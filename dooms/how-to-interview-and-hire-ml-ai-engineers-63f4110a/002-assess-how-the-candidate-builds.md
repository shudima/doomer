A practical coding exercise can reveal more than whether someone reaches a working output. Yan suggests examining decomposition, maintainability, edge cases, tests and responses to feedback.

Examples include an inference endpoint or a data-processing pipeline. The exercise should fit the role, while the interview gathers evidence about engineering habits instead of reducing the decision to completion speed alone.
