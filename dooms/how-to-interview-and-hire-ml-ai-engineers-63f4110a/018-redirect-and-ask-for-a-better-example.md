An interviewer's job includes managing the limited time. Yan recommends politely redirecting a response that drifts away from useful evidence rather than passively letting the conversation run out.

If the first example does not illuminate the relevant capability, another example may help. Having several ways to probe the same dimension can reveal strengths that the first question failed to surface.
