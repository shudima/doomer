Discovery, growth and mature operations need different leadership strengths. Some people learn to work across all three stages, but Yan cautions against assuming that everyone naturally excels at each one.

Know your strengths, develop weaker areas and work with people who complement you. Choose leadership responsibilities with the situation in mind. A leader who succeeds in one setting may need a different approach or different colleagues in another.
