**Illustrative example**

A team can finish a migration, but it cannot obtain the required cross-team access approval. Repeating “move faster” does not solve the blockage.

The leader finds the responsible owner, clarifies the requirement and helps reach a decision. Once the obstacle is removed, the team can carry on with the work it already knows how to do.
