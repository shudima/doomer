The soldier style suits rapid growth and expansion. These leaders turn early products into larger operations while balancing execution speed, complexity and enough stability to keep functioning.

Yan distinguishes this from both initial discovery and mature optimization. Scaling a working pilot introduces new conditions, so successful growth requires more than repeating the actions that produced the first small success.
