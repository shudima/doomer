A useful vision combines an ambitious direction with needs that are likely to remain important. Understanding what customers will continue to value can provide a firmer foundation than predicting every new trend.

Eugene Yan describes leaders communicating goals they believe in, including during difficulty. A credible direction attracts people who want to help. Vision includes both the change you want to create and the enduring needs that make it worthwhile.
