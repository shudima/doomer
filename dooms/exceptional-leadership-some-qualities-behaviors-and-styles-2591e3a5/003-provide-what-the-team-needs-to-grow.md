Leaders supply direction, context, motivation, resources, opportunities and coaching. Yan emphasizes enabling people rather than spoon-feeding each next action.

The practical question is whether the team has what it needs to succeed and keep improving. Providing more instructions is not always the missing contribution; access, judgment-building experiences or meaningful context may matter more.
