**Illustrative example**

An early product team needs experiments to discover what customers value. Later, a growing service needs dependable operations and clear ownership.

Keep asking which problem leadership must solve now. The habits that helped the team explore may need to change when consistency becomes the limiting factor. The example illustrates the article’s stage-based metaphors.
