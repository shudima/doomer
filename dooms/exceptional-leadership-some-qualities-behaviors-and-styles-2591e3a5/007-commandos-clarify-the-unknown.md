Yan's commando metaphor describes leaders suited to ambiguous, risky beginnings. They make decisions with incomplete information, learn quickly and change direction when reality contradicts the plan.

This style is especially useful when an organization is trying to discover what should exist. Its strength is rapid learning and movement from uncertainty toward a viable direction, rather than maintaining an already stable system.
