Politics, confusion and unnecessary bureaucracy can consume a team's attention without improving its work. Yan describes removing such obstacles as a core leadership responsibility.

The leader should address blockers that the team cannot resolve, or could resolve only at excessive cost. This protects focus and delivery while allowing the team to retain credit and ownership for the work itself.
