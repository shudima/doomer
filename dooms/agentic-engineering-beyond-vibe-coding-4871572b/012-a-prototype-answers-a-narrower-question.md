**Illustrative example**

A quick prototype shows whether users understand a new upload flow. It uses temporary storage and simplified errors.

That can be enough for the experiment. Before production, the team still needs durable storage, access controls and recovery behavior. Write down which question the prototype answered so its success does not silently stand in for a production review.
