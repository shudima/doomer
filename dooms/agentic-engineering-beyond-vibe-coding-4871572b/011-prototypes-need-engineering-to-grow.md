Vibe coding can make experimentation fast. Without structure, testing and architectural consistency, it can also accumulate technical debt quickly and make generated code difficult to maintain.

The video uses agentic engineering to emphasize the discipline needed as a project grows. The **scope** of engineering expands to another level of abstraction, with maintainability and quality still determining whether the result can support real use.
