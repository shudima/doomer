AI changes where engineering effort goes. You may write fewer individual lines and spend more time explaining the goal, shaping the environment and checking the result.

The video calls this agentic engineering. The work still needs clear requirements, useful tools and feedback. Generating a plausible implementation is one step in delivering software people can rely on.
