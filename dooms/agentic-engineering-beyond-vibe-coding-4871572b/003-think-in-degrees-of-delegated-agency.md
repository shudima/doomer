The video places development approaches on a spectrum of human control and delegated agency. It is not a ranking from best to worst.

At one end, engineers define almost every rule and implementation detail. AI-assisted coding adds completion, refactoring and generated snippets while the developer still drives the implementation. The distinction is how much decision-making the system receives, not whether any AI is present.
