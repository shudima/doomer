## Summary

Agentic engineering shifts work from specifying every implementation step to shaping and supervising probabilistic workflows. The video uses an agency spectrum, distinguishes prototype-oriented vibe coding from production discipline, and concludes that developers should experiment without giving up understanding or quality.
