**Illustrative example**

For a topic search, specify: match title text without case sensitivity, keep finished topics visible, and show a clear empty state.

Name the relevant component and the checks you expect. The agent now has a bounded task and you have observable behavior to review. “Add search” leaves several product decisions unstated.
