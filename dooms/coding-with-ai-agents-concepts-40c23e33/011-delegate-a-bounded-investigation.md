A subagent can investigate how a library works, test it, and report its findings to the main agent. That gives the larger task a way to draw on focused work without requiring one conversation to do everything itself.

The presenter places subagents alongside skills and automation as capabilities developers can add to their workflow. Their value depends on understanding what each delegated piece is meant to contribute.
