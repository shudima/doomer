Yan does not want every conversation to carry a full delivery workflow. For brainstorming and rough thinking, he sometimes uses a simpler mode with fewer agentic mechanisms.

The choice follows the purpose of the session. A system optimized for shipping checked artifacts can add unnecessary friction when the immediate goal is exploring an idea rather than completing production work.
