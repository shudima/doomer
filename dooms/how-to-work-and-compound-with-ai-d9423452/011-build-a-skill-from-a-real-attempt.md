Yan usually performs a task interactively before asking the assistant to turn that experience into a skill. Running the resulting procedure on another example exposes where it overfits the first session.

Corrections then improve the procedure. Examples of desired outputs can also help the assistant infer the conventions that would otherwise remain implicit in the user's judgment.
