**Verification** has different costs. Formatting and linting can run deterministically after an edit, while tests, evaluations and model reviews require more time or judgment.

Yan recommends catching an error on the cheapest suitable rung of that ladder. Early automated feedback prevents simple mistakes from consuming expensive reasoning or reaching a later review stage where they distract from harder questions.
