Yan uses completion sounds, descriptive window titles and status information to monitor several sessions. Remote access can help unblock urgent work when he is away from the computer.

The purpose is visibility rather than constant supervision. He explicitly cautions against turning every spare moment into checking sessions when the better choice is being present or taking a break.
