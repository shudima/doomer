Long tasks can drift in two ways. Execution drift means mishandling the intended task, such as ignoring an error or reporting a bad metric. Direction drift means pursuing the wrong interpretation of the task itself.

Yan describes a fresh-context reviewer checking the original specification against recent work. Tactical checks should be frequent; occasional broader checks ask whether the work still serves the original intent.
