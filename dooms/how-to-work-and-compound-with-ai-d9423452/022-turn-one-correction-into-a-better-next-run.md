**Illustrative example**

You repeatedly correct reports because their totals do not reconcile. Save a check in the reporting procedure and include the script that computes the totals.

On the next run, the assistant can catch the mistake before presenting the report. A correction has compounded when it improves the process, rather than remaining only in an old conversation.
