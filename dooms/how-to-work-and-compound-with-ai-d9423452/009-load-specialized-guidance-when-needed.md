A large instruction file imposes a recurring context cost, even when most of its material is irrelevant. Yan moves specialized guidance into separate files and tells the assistant when to consult them.

The distinction matters: automatically importing a guide merely moves the same always-loaded text elsewhere. Conditional reading can actually reduce the amount a session must carry.
