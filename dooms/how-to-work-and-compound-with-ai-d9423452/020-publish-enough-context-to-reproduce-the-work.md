Work left only in a person's head cannot help the next teammate or model session. Yan favors shared documents, repositories and channels that preserve decisions and point to completed artifacts.

His practical test is whether someone new could reproduce last week's work using the shared record. If they cannot, useful context may still be missing from the organization's accessible memory.
