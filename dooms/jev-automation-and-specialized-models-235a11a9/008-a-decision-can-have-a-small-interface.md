**Illustrative example**

A hypothetical mail sorter needs one of three actions: keep, archive or ask a person. Its caller may also need a calibrated probability.

That is a small typed decision interface. A paragraph explaining the email is a different output with different latency and interpretation costs. This example illustrates the architecture discussion; it is not a claim about a measured Jev result.
