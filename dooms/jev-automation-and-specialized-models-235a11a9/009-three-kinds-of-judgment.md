The video describes three primitives: choosing a category, scoring along an ordered set of choices, and estimating the probability of a yes/no answer.

These become building blocks for larger applications. Caleb compares the experience to working with lower-level components and constructing abstractions above them. A compact decision output still needs surrounding software to turn it into a useful workflow.
